# LivePull brand

| Colour | Hex |
|---|---|
| Deep navy | `#08264B` |

- `icon.svg` – square app icon on navy (WordPress.org icon, favicons).
- `mark.svg` – the mark on its own, for light backgrounds, next to the wordmark.
- Wordmark: Inter Display ExtraBold, "Live" in navy and "Pull" in blue.

The two windows are the live site and your local copy, the arrow is the one-way pull, and the shield is your local work being protected.
